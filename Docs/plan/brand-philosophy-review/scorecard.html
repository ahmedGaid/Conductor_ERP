Session A findings — full pass (2026-07-20)

App frame and global states, driven live in Arabic/RTL and English/LTR, light and dark: login, app shell, sidebar, sticky header, ⌘K palette, notifications, the dashboard's loading and loaded states, and the unknown-route path. Ranked most severe first. Nothing here is fixed — each becomes its own session.

P1 · dashboard

A negative cash balance looks exactly like a healthy one

The Cash Balance KPI reads -1,390,234.86 EGP in the same plain near-white as Total Revenue — no colour, no icon, no word. Meanwhile a +903.3% profit delta gets green and a +25.8% expense delta gets red. Colour is spent on the movement and withheld from the number that would actually alarm an owner; the minus sign is the only signal, and it is easy to miss at that size. The one screen that exists to tell the owner where the business stands stays silent on its worst number.

Fails Standard 8 + 3 · judgment call on severity — filed P1 because it defeats the dashboard's whole purpose
P2 · routing

An unknown route silently becomes Home — there is no not-found state

Navigating to #/definitely-not-a-real-route rewrites the URL to #/ and renders the dashboard, with "الرئيسية" marked active in the nav. A mistyped address, a stale bookmark or a link to a since-renamed screen all end the same way: the user is somewhere they did not ask to be, with nothing saying so. The no-permission path very likely lands here too, since nav is permission-filtered and unmatched routes fall through to the same catch-all.

Fails Standard 4 + 8 · breaks the designed-states rule (no not-found state exists at all)
P2 · i18n · global

No plural forms anywhere — Arabic counts read wrong

48 Arabic and 51 English keys interpolate {{count}}, and there is not a single plural-form key (_one/_two/_few/…) in either locale. Arabic has six plural categories; every one of these strings is wrong at most counts. Live on the dashboard: "1 رسائل فشل إرسالها" — should be "رسالة واحدة". English is wrong too ("1 messages failed to send"). The import flow hand-rolled *One variants, which proves the team knows the problem, but the fix never generalized. This is precisely the native-Arabic craft the brand claims as its moat.

Fails Standard 7 · Identity §6 · key seen live: dashboard.attention.failedMessages
P2 · global

One generic list skeleton stands in for every page

App.tsx uses <Suspense fallback={<ListSkeleton rows={6}/>}> for every lazy route, and DashboardPage renders the same component while loading. So a dashboard of four KPI cards plus two panels first paints as five stacked full-width bars, then jumps to a completely different layout. The skeleton never describes the page it is standing in for, which makes the wait feel longer, not shorter, and produces a visible jolt on arrival.

Fails Standard 2 + 7 · App.tsx Suspense fallback + DashboardPage.tsx:104
P2 · dashboard

The greeting calls the owner by his system login

DashboardPage.tsx:92 passes data.username into the greeting, so the first line of the product every morning is "صباح الخير، admin" — an English system identifier dropped into an Arabic sentence. The display name is already known and correct: the header two rows above shows "Ahmed Gaid". In English it is no better: "Good morning, admin".

Fails Standard 7 · use prefs.display_name, fall back to username
P2 · notifications

Every row offers Resend, including the ones that succeeded

The delivery log paints a persistent bordered "إعادة الإرسال" button on all ~12 visible rows, every one of them already مُرسَل. The result is a column of identical repeat-send buttons down the edge of the screen — visually the loudest thing on the page, and an invitation to double-send a customer invoice by mistake. Resend belongs on failed rows, or on hover.

Fails Standard 1 + 3 · reversibility concern under Standard 4
P2 · notifications

Email subjects render as raw English inside the Arabic UI

The الموضوع column shows Invoice JE-2026-000271 verbatim on every row of an otherwise fully Arabic screen. The subjects are generated English-only, so the Arabic-first promise breaks at exactly the point the customer sees what was sent on their behalf. Pairs with the notification-body localization gap already filed as finding #4.

Fails Standard 7 · Identity §6
P2 · dashboard

Good and bad movement share one glyph — only colour tells them apart

Total Expenses shows ▲ 25.8% in red and Net Profit shows ▲ 903.3% in green. Same arrow, same direction, opposite meanings, distinguished by hue alone. The brand rule is that colour always pairs with a word or an icon that carries the meaning by itself — here the icon is identical in both cases, so a red-green colourblind owner reads the two cards the same way.

Fails Standard 3 (colour must earn its place) · a11y
P2 · dashboard

"System confidence" reports a problem it gives you no way to fix

The panel lists Backups not confirmed with a warning icon and then stops — no link, no chevron, no next step, unlike the "Needs attention" rows directly above it which all carry one. A panel whose job is confidence tells the owner something is wrong and leaves them holding it.

Fails Standard 8 + 4
P3 · global

A focus box is painted around the page title on every navigation

The <h1> carries tabIndex=-1 and receives focus on route change — the right accessibility instinct — but the outline is not scoped to :focus-visible, so mouse users see a bright rectangle drawn around the heading every single time they change page. It reads as a rendering fault.

Fails Standard 7 · scope the outline to :focus-visible
P3 · dashboard

The page title changes identity mid-load

DashboardPage.tsx:90-94 renders "الرئيسية" while loading and swaps it for "صباح الخير، …" once data lands. The h1 is the page's anchor; having it rename itself a second after arrival is a small, repeated instability.

Fails Standard 5 + 2
P3 · tokens

Dark mode is pure black, while the token file promises near-black

tokens.css describes dark mode as "a near-black canvas" in its own comment at line 188, then sets --color-bg: #000000 at line 196 — the one value that is not near-black. Light mode is correctly a tint (--palette-ink-050), not pure white, so the asymmetry is unintended. Small, but tokens.css is the single authority: either the value or the comment must move.

Fails Standard 7 · tokens.css:188 vs :196
P3 · ⌘K palette

The palette drops the icons the sidebar uses for the same destinations

Every "الانتقال إلى" entry is text-only, while the sidebar shows each of those exact destinations with its icon. The same thing is presented two ways in two places, so the palette loses the fast visual recognition the icon set was built to give.

Fails Standard 5
P3 · sidebar

The nav advertises an unbuilt module and an internal ops screen

"وحدات قادمة → التقارير · قريبًا" ships a permanently disabled row in the customer's main navigation, and "صحة المساعد" (Assistant health) puts an internal diagnostics screen alongside Users and Roles. Both add noise to the frame, and the first is a soft claim about something that has not shipped.

Fails Standard 1 + 3 · Brief §12 claims discipline
P3 · notifications

The page's only primary button is an export, and it is labelled "PDF / طباعة"

The single filled near-black button on the delivery log is the print/export action — the primary slot spent on a utility rather than on what the page is for. The label itself splices a Latin acronym and an Arabic word with a slash, against the one-canonical- word rule. The المرجع and الموضوع columns also repeat the same reference on every row.

Fails Standard 1 · Identity §6
P3 · login

No way back in if the owner forgets the password

The login card offers username, password, submit — and nothing else. For a product about to be handed to a customer who will administer it themselves, a locked-out owner has no self-serve path and no on-screen hint of who to ask. Also minor: a long money value in a KPI card wraps to a second line and breaks the alignment of the KPI row (seen at 1440 px on Cash Balance).

Fails Standard 8 · may be a deliberate admin-provisioned decision — confirm with founder before filing a fix

Session B findings — Accounting (2026-07-20)

14 accounting routes driven live in Arabic/RTL and English/LTR, light and dark: chart of accounts, journals, new entry, trial balance, general ledger, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, VAT return, fixed assets, cost centers, bank reconciliation, budgets, report builder. Ranked most severe first.

P1 · module-wide

The chart of accounts is English-only, by construction

An Egyptian accountant opens the finance module and reads Cash · Bank · Accounts Receivable · VAT Input (Recoverable) · Accumulated Depreciation — 27 accounts, all English, next to a النوع column that is correctly Arabic. This is not demo data: erp/accounting/services/seeding.py calls itself "the single source of truth" and the first-run setup wizard runs it, so a real customer is provisioned this way. The names then propagate into every report — trial balance, general ledger, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow all render them verbatim. The fix is structural, not a string swap: Account has a single name field (domain/models.py:21) with no Arabic counterpart, so bilingual account names need a model change plus a migration for existing installs.

Fails Standard 7 + 8 · Identity §6 · the deepest Arabic-first breach found so far
P1 · report builder

Saturated red Delete buttons, one click, on every saved-report row

Each row carries a filled bright-red حذف button sitting directly beside a filled black تشغيل. It is the loudest colour anywhere in the product, it is permanently visible in a list rather than revealed on intent, it is destructive, and nothing on screen says what will happen before it happens. Nowhere else in the app is a row-level destructive action styled as a solid red button — so it breaks consistency as well as calm. This is the clearest "Linear would not ship this" on the surface.

Fails Standard 3 + 4 + 5 · violates the monochrome-chrome and colour-earns-its-place rules at once
P2 · general ledger

The general ledger's empty state is one grey line of text

Before an account is chosen, the page shows "اختر حسابًا" — small, grey, floating in an otherwise blank area — which is the exact same string already sitting in the select's placeholder directly above it. No icon, no explanation of what the ledger will show, no suggested or recent accounts. The module's most-used drill-down screen greets you with a bare state and says the same thing twice.

Fails Standard 7 · breaks the designed-states rule
P2 · new journal entry

An untouched blank form already accuses you of being unbalanced

Opening "قيد جديد" shows غير متوازن in red against empty totals of 0.00 / 0.00 — before a single keystroke. A blank form is not an error; showing a red failure on arrival is the opposite of blame-free, and it trains the user to ignore the one indicator that matters when it turns red for real.

Fails Standard 4 + 7 · blame-free-error rule
P2 · new journal entry

Posting to the ledger is the only way to submit — there is no draft

The single submit control is ترحيل القيد, which writes the general ledger. There is no "save as draft", so a half-finished entry is either abandoned or posted. Worth weighing against the guard work already shipped in agent-posting-plan FILE_01: the assistant is gated behind a typed-retype challenge before it may post, while the manual form posts on one click with no confirm step at all.

Fails Standard 4 (reversible; say what happens before)
P2 · navigation

Two tabs light up as active at the same time

On /accounting/journals/new both القيود and قيد جديد carry the active underline simultaneously. Every other accounting route marks exactly one. The user cannot tell where they are from the nav.

Fails Standard 5 + 4
P2 · navigation

The breadcrumb lies on the second row of tabs

On Bank Reconciliation and Report Builder the breadcrumb still reads المالية › دليل الحسابات while the active tab is elsewhere. The first-row tabs (Income Statement, Trial Balance, VAT return) update it correctly, so this is scoped to the wrapped second row — a stale value, not a missing one, which is worse: it states a location confidently and is wrong.

Fails Standard 4 + 5
P2 · module front door

Fourteen equal-weight tabs wrapped onto two rows

The module opens on a strip of 14 tabs — chart of accounts, journals, new entry, trial balance, general ledger, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, VAT return, fixed assets, cost centers, bank reconciliation, budgets, report builder — none grouped, none ranked, wrapping to a second line at 1440 px. Nothing tells a new user where to start, which is the opposite of "real work within minutes, without training". Compounding it, قيد جديد is both a tab and a primary button in the Journals page header: the same action in two places, styled two ways.

Fails Standard 1 + 5 · Brief §14
P2 · module-wide

Pages open in data-entry mode with the create form already expanded

Chart of Accounts, Bank Reconciliation and Report Builder each devote the top of the page to a permanently open create form, before the user has asked to create anything. The content they came for is pushed down, and the screen's first impression is a row of empty inputs rather than the books. Three screens do it the same way, so it is a pattern, not an oversight — which also means one fix covers all three.

Fails Standard 1 + 3

"EGP" is printed on every single cell

The trial balance repeats the currency 26 times on one screen; the income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and VAT return all do the same. The ledger is EGP-only (base_currency is read-only in the UI), so the code is carrying no information and is actively making the columns harder to scan and compare. Session C findings — Inventory (2026-07-20)

Six inventory routes plus three detail screens driven live in Arabic/RTL and English/LTR, dark and light: stock on hand, items, item detail, warehouses, warehouse detail, movements, stock counts, count detail, batches. Ranked most severe first.

P1 · batches

The batch screen cannot answer the only question batch tracking exists to answer

Batch and lot tracking exists so a food or pharmacy business knows what expires next. The page shows an Earliest expiry column rendered as inert grey text — the same weight as every other cell — and nothing else. There is no days-remaining figure, no emphasis on a batch expiring soon, no default sort by expiry, no "expiring this month" filter, and no summary. An owner cannot look at this screen and learn anything they could not read off a spreadsheet. The page also has no primary action of any kind: it is a read-only table with a bare All / Filter toolbar above it. For the Egyptian SMBs this product is aimed at, this is the module's weakest screen.

Fails Standard 1 + 8 · the page has no job and leaves the owner no more informed than before
P1 · stock count detail

The count's variance is hidden until after you have already posted it

A stock count exists to surface the difference between what the system thinks you hold and what you actually counted. On this screen the variance columns are literally not rendered until the count is posted — {posted && <th>…variance…} (StockCountDetailPage.tsx:127-128) — so the user commits an irreversible stock adjustment and a general-ledger entry before ever being shown what it will change. Post count sits in the sticky header, always enabled, with no confirmation and no preview. Posting a sheet where nothing was counted still succeeds and still shows a success toast: post_count skips lines whose counted_quantity is null (erp/inventory/services/stock_count.py:77) and then locks the count anyway. The data is safe; the promise is not.

Fails Standard 4 + 8 · says nothing before, reassures nothing after, and is not reversible
P2 · movements

Stock in and stock out look exactly the same

In the movements ledger Receive and Issue (استلام / صرف) are plain text in the same weight and colour, and the quantity column is unsigned — a receipt of 200 and an issue of 5 both read as bare positive numbers. There is no glyph, no sign, no alignment cue. Scanning the stock ledger for "what left the warehouse this week" means reading every row's first column word by word. Confirmed identical in Arabic and English, dark and light. Session A logged the dashboard version of this (one glyph, colour only); here there is not even colour.

Fails Standard 1 + 7 · the ledger's most important distinction is the least visible thing on the screen
P2 · module-wide

Every list page opens in data-entry mode, with the create form already expanded

Items, warehouses, movements and stock counts all push their create form to the top of the page, permanently open, before the user has asked to create anything. On movements this is worse than clutter: the always-visible form's primary button is Post movement, which writes stock and a journal entry with no confirmation step. Session B found this exact pattern across Accounting; finding it again across a second whole module makes it systemic rather than local, and the fix should be one shared decision, not per-page patches.

Fails Standard 1 + 4 + 5 · the page's first impression is a form, not the work
P2 · stock counts

Two different date formats on one screen, in the same field

The Count date picker renders 20/07/2026 while the Count date column of the table directly below it renders 2026-06-19. Same screen, same concept, two formats — day-first in the control, ISO in the data. Confirmed in both locales (Arabic shows 2026/07/20 against 2026-06-19). Dates are formatted per component rather than through one shared edge formatter, the way money already is.

Fails Standard 5 + 7 · breaks the one-formatter discipline that lib/money.ts already models
P2 · stock counts

A cyan status badge, in a product that has no cyan

The Counting / جارٍ الجرد badge renders sky blue (rgb(56, 189, 248) on a cyan tint) — a hue that appears nowhere else in the module, in either theme, and that has no defined meaning in the identity system. It is the single most saturated thing on the page and it sits next to a monochrome table. It reads as a leftover from a starter palette rather than a decision. Session B found the same class of problem in Accounting ("a new colour with no defined meaning"), which suggests the status-badge tones need one audit rather than two fixes.

Fails Standard 3 · colour that does not earn its place
P2 · stock count detail

You cannot correct a counted quantity once you have typed one

saveLine begins if (value === "" || value === current) return; (StockCountDetailPage.tsx:44). Typing 500 into a line, realising it is wrong, clearing the box and tabbing away silently leaves 500 stored — the field is cleared on screen but the empty value is never sent, and the old number returns on reload. During a physical stock count, mistyping and clearing is the single most likely user action on the screen. Nothing tells the user the correction did not take.

Fails Standard 4 + 8 · silent data loss on the most common correction path
P2 · item detail

The item page shows you everything and lets you do nothing

Opening an item gives unit, type, stock value, stock by warehouse and recent movements — and not one action. You cannot rename the item, adjust its stock, or start a movement for it. The Recent movements table does not link through to the movements ledger filtered to this item, so the five rows shown are the end of the road. The warehouse detail page has the same shape. Every path out of these screens goes backwards.

Fails Standard 1 + 8 · a detail screen with no primary action
P2 · module-wide

The module blurb repeats on every screen, including deep detail pages

"Items, warehouses, current stock levels and every movement in and out." sits under the title Inventory on all nine screens driven, never changing — including the stock count detail page, where the user is mid-task on one warehouse's count and is told again what the inventory module is. What this surface gets right — do not "fix" these

The user guide is the best writing in the product. Ten task-shaped journeys (إصدار أول فاتورة, أرصدة بداية الشهر, معالجة موافقة مرفوضة), each with steps in the order a person actually performs them, and — rare anywhere — a ما الذي قد يحدث خطأ section that names the two most likely failures and the exact recovery for each, blame-free. The glossary is a moat asset: thirty-eight terms defined in one plain Egyptian sentence apiece, including the hard ones (ترحيل, تسوية vs مطابقة, يستحق قريبًا) — one authoritative entry points at the wrong sales word, filed above, and the rest is a model of what Identity §6 wants. The Help Center panel knows where you are: opened on the sales list it answers purpose, mechanism, steps and related pages for that screen, in four short blocks, no scrolling. The knowledge base page is honest and calm — it states plainly what the assistant can search and quote, and shows per- document indexing status rather than pretending upload equals readiness. The assistant's manners are right: the empty state names its scope and grounds it in the user's own records rather than promising intelligence, the four suggestion chips are real questions an owner has, and the working state is a pulsing plan step with a visible إيقاف — it shows what it is about to do and lets you stop it, which is the behaviour Standard 6 is asking for.
