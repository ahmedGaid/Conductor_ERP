# Support & Warranty Terms

This page summarizes the support and warranty relationship for Conductor ERP.
It is a plain-language summary, not a legal contract — the binding terms are
whatever is agreed in the signed service agreement between the customer and
Ahmed Gaid ("the vendor"). If this page and the service agreement disagree,
the service agreement wins.

## License

This software is proprietary — see [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) at the repo root.
It is licensed for the customer's own internal use on infrastructure the
customer controls, not sold or open for redistribution.

## Support scope

Exact response times, hours of coverage, and channels (email/phone/on-site)
are to be agreed in the service contract with each customer. Until that
contract is signed, no specific support-level commitment is made here.

## Update channel

Updates ship as new versions of this codebase (see [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md)
and `manage.py upgrade` in [`Docs/RUNBOOK.md`](RUNBOOK.md)). Whether updates are
pushed proactively or applied on request is part of the service agreement.

## Warranty

No warranty is made beyond what is explicitly stated in the signed service
agreement. The software is provided "as is" absent that agreement — see
[`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

## Data ownership

The customer owns their business data. The software is customer-hosted —
data stays on infrastructure the customer controls.
