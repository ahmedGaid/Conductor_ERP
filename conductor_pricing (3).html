Questions before you start

How does pricing work?
A one-time implementation fee (setup, migration, training) plus a simple per-user monthly subscription. You own your setup and subscribe to the platform.
Is there a free trial?
No free plan. We run a guided demo and a scoped pilot, so you see Conductor working with your own processes before you commit.
Can different people have different modules?
Yes. Pick a tier for a standard bundle, or ask about a custom plan to price each user by the exact modules they use.
Can I change plans later?
Anytime. Move between tiers or adjust modules per user — your monthly bill updates the next cycle.
Do I save by paying yearly?
Yes. Annual billing gives you roughly one month free (about 10% off the monthly price).
